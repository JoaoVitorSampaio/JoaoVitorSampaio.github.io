João Vitor Sampaio Costa

Computer Science · UFPB · João Pessoa, Brazil

I am a seventh-semester Computer Science undergraduate at the Federal University of Paraíba, in Brazil. What interests me is applied data science: models that have to hold up against real data, with real people using the output on the other end.

My research is on sports prediction using logic programming combined with machine learning, which was the subject of my first published paper. I work on the TV 3.0 Test Suite at LAVID, hold a research scholarship at PET Saúde Digital on a perinatal telehealth project with the Brazilian Ministry of Health, and serve on the computer vision board of TAIL, the first academic AI league in the Brazilian Northeast. In September 2026 I start as a full stack intern at CODATA, the state data processing company of Paraíba.

I was selling things before university. I started JVC Sports, a sportswear store in Patos, at 16. It began online, grew into a physical shop, closed when I moved to João Pessoa to study, and reopened in 2026. I like running, cycling, training and watching football, which is how football ended up becoming the subject of my research projects.

You can reach me at joaovitorsampai@gmail.com, browse my GitHub, or connect on LinkedIn.

Research

Score Predictor: Predição de Resultados no Futebol Usando Programação Lógica

Logic programmingPrologSports prediction

Oliveira, G. B. R.; Costa, J. V. S.; Silva, T. B.; Simões, T. A.
6th Brazilian Logic Workshop (WBL), CSBC 2025, Maceió, Brazil. SBC, pp. 24-31.

A match outcome prediction system for the Brazilian Serie A, built on a base of declarative rules in Prolog that estimates win, draw and loss probabilities from recent form, attacking and defensive strength, and missing first-choice players. It started as a course final project and was presented at the conference.

Selected projects

Score Predictor

PrologMachine LearningFootball

A prediction system for the Brazilian Serie A, currently at version 3.2. Beyond generating forecasts automatically and locking them before kickoff, it compares its own probabilities against bookmaker implied odds across the season. That turns evaluation into an honest test rather than a single accuracy figure.

[System] · [Paper]

Football Player Recommendation System

Recommender systemsFBrefxG

A transfer recommendation system in the spirit of Sensible Transfers, developed at TAIL. Starting from a squad tactical need and style of play, it searches for candidates across different leagues and applies similarity techniques to find the best fitting players, producing a full scouting report.

Valora.ai

RegressionSHAPInterpretability

Property price prediction with machine learning. Accuracy is not the only goal: I use permutation importance and SHAP values to isolate the real effect of each attribute and to investigate collinearity between variables such as number of en-suite rooms, floor area and neighbourhood.

Inventory and sales system for JVC Sports

ReactSupabaseLLM

A system I built for my own shop: inventory and sales control with an SKU-grouped product view, a sale detail modal and AI-generated suggestions. It is the project where the client, the requirements and the developer are the same person, which forced me to decide what actually mattered instead of listing features.

RealEstate Expert

Knowledge GraphGraphRAGLangGraph

An expert system on the Joao Pessoa property market, combining a knowledge graph in Memgraph/Cypher, GraphRAG, document RAG in ChromaDB and LLM orchestration with LangGraph, served through FastAPI. It includes a Cypher validator as an anti-hallucination contract.
